Boards, to scale

Every card drawn 1 : 1 from its own document’s parts list, 100 mm high, each taking the shortest of 12, 18 or 24 cm that its packages fit. Apple II proportions: gold fingers along the bottom rear, connectors on the back edge. Click any board to read it full size.

The motherboard carries four system-RAM footprints — one fitted, three reserved — and the drawing is where their two preconditions become visible.

Three of five cards are the short one

Only video needs 24 cm and only audio needs 18. Net, storage and the combined I/O card all fit 12 — and net is as dense at 12 cm (64 %) as video is at 24. Five cards come to 780 cm² of board against 1,250 if every card were cut to the longest.

PS/2 and serial, combined

The two smallest cards merge cleanly, and the reason is in the $FF map rather than in the logic: PS/2 holds $FF50–$FF53 and serial $FF54–$FF57, which are contiguous. One card decodes eight bytes where two cards decoded four each.

Both were already /IRQ sources and both keep their place in machine.md §4.1’s polling order — serial stays last, because reading the 6551’s STATUS clears the interrupt and returns the error bits in the same read. Merging the cards does not merge the handlers.

Drawn at 14 ICs, which is 11 + 3 with nothing shared. The obvious saving is the second GAL22V10: two four-byte decodes become one eight-byte decode. It is not free — ps2.md §9 already calls its GAL the fitting risk at roughly ten macrocells of ten, and the pressure is the DR latches and /PL terms rather than the decode. A single ATF1508AS would absorb both GALs, the '273 and the '244 and take the card to 11, which the retired house rule no longer forbids. Neither is specified here; the drawing shows the honest 14.

And a slot comes back

Six cards became five, so one of the six slots is now spare — drawn empty. That matters to an argument on the books: net.md §12 justifies the net card’s CPLDs partly on “six slots, five of them already claimed — there is one”, and a 74xx MAC needing two slots was the reason it lost. The premise has changed even though the conclusion has not; the CPLD case now rests on the CRC arithmetic alone.

The dashed outlines are the card envelopes at their real lengths. A socket is 91.4 mm and the longest card is 240, so video overhangs its socket by 149 mm while the three 12 cm cards barely overhang at all. The board is sized by the longest card; the motherboard’s own logic sits in a strip outside every envelope.

The SRAM bank — one populated, three reserved

Four AS6C4008 footprints in a row, U8 fitted and RAM2–RAM4 left empty, plus a reserved 74HC139. They cost 27 cm² of board and nothing else while unpopulated, and the board grows from 176 to 190 mm to carry them. Two things have to be true before any of them can be stuffed, and neither is true today.

⚠ 1. Only two of the three have an address to live at. machine.md §7.1 fixes system RAM at A20 = 0, A19 = 0. Three more parts want 1.5 MB, and the 2 MB map is already spoken for:

The megabyte at A20 = 1 would take two of them and evict every card buffer — which is what retired the TFM hazard on two cards four days ago. The third has nowhere at all short of moving VRAM. Reserving the footprints is free; populating them is a map decision nobody has made.

⚠ 2. The decode is the part §7.1 deleted. One SRAM needs no decode: /CE is /IOPAGE · /A19 · /A20 and U6 already forms it. Four need A20:A19 split into four chip selects, and U6 is 10 macrocells of 10 with none spare — so it is a 74HC139, drawn reserved beside the bank. §7.1’s own words: “the decode was a part the fourth SRAM created and the first one never needed.” Reserving the fourth socket reserves it again.

Placeable area is the board less the 91.4 × 11 mm finger zone and the reserved rear connectors, so it differs per card. A 12 cm card gives up proportionally more to the fingers than a 24 cm one — which is why net reads 64 % on a board only a third the size of video’s.

What this drawing is, and is not

It is every package from each card’s own chip budget at its real DIP or PLCC footprint, placed by a skyline packer — parts settle to the lowest free position rather than into fixed rows, so a pair of 0.3″ DIPs can stack beside a PLCC the way they would on a real through-hole board. Courtyards are 0.1″ on every side. Each document’s connectors and analogue corners are reserved on the rear edge first.

It is not a layout. No traces, no vias, no decoupling capacitors — about thirty per card, another 6 cm² the percentages do not carry. Real placement groups by signal, not by what fits lowest. The area figure is the finding; the arrangement illustrates it.
